# Strawberry Overnight Oats

## Ingredients
- 1 cup whole milk plain yogurt
- 1/3 cup instant oats (or oat flour)
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 2 tablespoons shredded unsweetened coconut
- 2 tablespoons hemp seeds
- 1 to 2 teaspoons maple syrup
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup diced strawberries

## Steps
1. Stir together the yogurt, oats, vanilla, milk, coconut, hemp seeds, and maple syrup.
2. Layer in jars or storage containers with the berries. Refrigerate overnight and serve cold.
