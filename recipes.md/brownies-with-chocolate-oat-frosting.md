# Brownies with Chocolate Oat Frosting

## Ingredients
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tablespoons ground flaxseed
- 3 tablespoons brewer's yeast
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup melted coconut oil or butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar (or coconut sugar)
- 1/4 cup honey or maple syrup
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 1/2 cup rolled oats, finely ground in a blender or food processor
- 3/4 cup dark chocolate chips

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Line an 8x8-inch pan with parchment paper.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, flaxseed, brewer's yeast, baking powder, and salt.
3. In a large bowl, whisk melted coconut oil or butter, sugar, honey, eggs, vanilla, and milk until smooth.
4. Add dry ingredients to wet and mix just until combined.
5. Fold in ground oats and dark chocolate chips.
6. Pour into the pan and spread evenly.
7. Bake for 22-26 minutes, until a toothpick comes out mostly clean (fudgy is good).
8. Cool completely before frosting.

# Chocolate Oat Frosting
## Ingredients
- 3 tablespoons butter, softened
- 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon milk, plus more as needed
- 1 tablespoon oat flour or finely ground oats

## Steps
1. Beat butter until creamy.
2. Add cocoa powder, powdered sugar, and vanilla, and mix until combined.
3. Add milk and combine. Add more milk 1 teaspoon at a time until frosting is smooth and spreadable.
4. Mix in oat flour.
5. Spread over cooled brownies and refrigerate 15 minutes to set.
