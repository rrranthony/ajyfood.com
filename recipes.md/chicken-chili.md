# Chicken Chili

## Ingredients
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 15-oz cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (or navy, great northern, or butter beans, or chickpeas)
- 1 16-oz jar salsa verde (medium or spicy)
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 bunch curly kale, de-ribbed and chopped
- 1 16-oz bag frozen white corn
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese, plus more for serving
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 lime, cut into wedges (for serving)
- Sour cream, hot sauce, tortilla chips, Fritos, or cornbread (optional, for serving)

## Steps
1. Combine the stock, chicken, beans, salsa verde, cumin, salt, oregano, onion powder, garlic powder, and chili powder in a large pot.
2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium-low.
3. Cover and cook until the chicken is cooked through, about 30 minutes.
4. Transfer the chicken to a bowl and shred it with two forks.
5. Return the shredded chicken to the pot.
6. Add the kale, corn, cream, cheese, and lime juice.
7. Cook uncovered until the kale is wilted and the chili has thickened, about 5 minutes.
8. Season with salt to taste.
9. Serve topped with the lime wedges, more cheddar, and any toppings you like.

## Notes
- Serves 6 to 8.
