# Recipe Style Guide

## Formatting
- No single-character fraction glyphs (½, ¼, etc.) — write `1/2`, `1/4`.
- Spell out units: "tablespoons", "teaspoons", not abbreviations.

## Steps
- One action per step. No compounding ("do this and then this").
- Keep steps concise.

## Ingredients
- Skip fresh herbs unless critical to the dish — use the dried equivalent instead.
- Prefer frozen corn over fresh when only kernels are needed, unless fresh really matters to the result.
- Leave out red pepper flakes unless the recipe depends on the heat.
- Omit salt and black pepper from the ingredient list unless a specific quantity matters — instead reference them in steps (e.g. "season with salt to taste").
